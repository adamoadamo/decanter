Yeobgamer is now called Decanter, with a new icon. Your games, settings and saves come with you.

- Decanter now chooses how to run each game. If a game closes or crashes as it starts, it tries another way by itself and remembers what works.
- Unity games that crashed as soon as they started now run, including games made with Unity 6.
- Games that start from a small launcher, including every Ren'Py visual novel, now stay "Playing" while you play, so Stop and ⌘Q work.
- A new Graphics option gives games made with DirectX 10 or 11 a second way to draw.
- The log under each game is now tucked away until you open it.
- Decanter tells you when a new version is out. You can also choose Decanter → Check for Updates….
- If your game library can't be read, Decanter keeps a copy of it instead of starting over.
