Yeobgamer is now called Decanter. Your games, settings and saves come with you.

- Unity games that crashed as soon as they started, such as Art Sqool and Sewer Rave, now run.
- Games that start from a small launcher, including every Ren'Py visual novel, now stay "Playing" while you play, so Stop and ⌘Q work.
- Decanter now tells you when a new version is out. You can also choose Decanter → Check for Updates….
- If your game library can't be read, Decanter keeps a copy of it instead of starting over.
